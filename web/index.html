The real cost

What breaks when you track more than memory allows

One door fits in a notebook. At two or three, move-out weeks collide: wrong surrender date, no dated photos at move-in, itemization scribbled after the clock already ran. Illinois gives you 30 days after surrender statewide; 45 in Chicago under RLTO. Counsel can explain penalties. Our job is the paper trail so you are not guessing on day 29.

$17/mo · $49/yr Pro is smaller than a single cleaning bill you lose in a dispute, or the stress of explaining why the deposit went back late. Most operators already use Google Calendar and Sheets; Deposit Desk adds one-click deadline and row export on step 5.
