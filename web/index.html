FAQ

Quick answers

Per turn or Pro?

One or two turns a year: plan to charge a per-turn documentation fee (like screening). Three or more turns across up to four doors: Pro annual usually wins on math.

Is Pro worth it vs one mistake?

One missed deadline or disputed withhold without move-in proof usually costs more than a year of Pro · or a single per-turn fee.

Property management software?

No. Deposit move-in/out paperwork, deadlines, and export only.

Outside Illinois?

Wizard supports Illinois (including Chicago RLTO), Indiana, Ohio, Michigan, Iowa, and Missouri default return clocks. Wisconsin and other states are not in the app yet. Local ordinances may differ · confirm with counsel.

Missed the deposit return deadline?

Pick a defensible surrender date, itemize withholds by line (not one lump “cleaning”), and export a dated packet. Start with Illinois missed deadline or Midwest roundup.

Tenant disputing the withhold?

Move-in proof, room tables, and wear vs damage notes matter more than arguing in email. See deposit dispute guide and no move-in photos.

Spreadsheet or Deposit Desk?

Sheets break when surrender dates collide across units. Compare packet vs spreadsheet and common Excel mistakes.
