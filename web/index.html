When does the deposit return clock start?

On surrender · keys back and the unit vacant · not the lease end date alone. Deposit Desk step 2 records that date for the deadline math. Read surrender date and keys not returned (concepts apply in every shipped state).

Which states are in the wizard?

18 states + DC with default 30- or 45-day return windows from surrender. Use the color bar above · gold = 45-day defaults · blue = 30-day · Ctrl+click (Mac: ⌘+click) a chip to open that state’s official deposit statute. Illinois adds a Chicago RLTO city toggle. Local ordinances may still differ · confirm with counsel. Browse guides by state or suggest a ruleset.

How long do I have to return a deposit?

State law (and sometimes city law) sets the window after surrender. Examples: Illinois statewide 765 ILCS 715/ · 30 days; Chicago RLTO · 45 days; Ohio ORC 5321.16 · 30 days. The wizard shows your state’s cite with a link · pick your state in Deposit Desk.

Per turn or Pro subscription?

Draft free in the browser. Per turn: $29 move-out print unlock or $49 full tenancy on one packet · often passed through like screening. Pro: $22/mo or $99/yr · unlimited print · up to 40 units · best when several turns stack each year. Full math on Pricing.

Is Deposit Desk property management software?

No. Move-in checklist, move-out line items, statutory deadline helper, Google Calendar / Sheets export, and print-ready packets only · not rent collection, listings, or maintenance ticketing.

Missed the deposit return deadline?

Lock a defensible surrender date, itemize withholds by line (not one lump “cleaning”), and export a dated packet · late documentation beats silence. Start with Illinois missed deadline, multi-state roundup, or your state cluster on Guides. Confirm penalties with counsel.

Tenant disputing the withhold?

Move-in room logs, dated photos, and line-item withholds beat email arguments. See deposit dispute guide, wear vs damage, and no move-in photos.

Spreadsheet or Deposit Desk?

Sheets work for one door · they break when surrender dates and cites differ across units in the same week. Compare packet vs spreadsheet and Excel mistakes.
