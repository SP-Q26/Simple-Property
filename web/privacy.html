Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Simple Property Tools (“we”) operates Deposit Desk at simple-property.com.

What we collect

Packet contents (tenant names, addresses, checklists) are stored in your browser’s localStorage when you use the wizard. We do not receive those fields on our servers unless you use optional email features below.

Operator logs (maintenance, tickets, inspections/complaints at /logs) are also stored in localStorage on your device unless you export CSV or print.

Tenant copy email (Pro) · If you send a statement copy to a tenant, we transmit the summary you submit (names, amounts, line items, deadline text) through our email provider (Resend) to the addresses you enter. We do not attach move-in photos in that email.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive subscription status, customer email from checkout metadata, and session identifiers · not your full card number.

Subscription index (Pro) · When configured, we store Stripe customer id, subscription status, and checkout email in encrypted Redis (Vercel/Upstash) so you can restore Pro on a new device via magic link.

Deadline reminders (optional) · If you schedule email reminders, we store your email, property label, and reminder dates until sent or expired.

Usage analytics

On our hosted site we use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights (first-party, cookie-light page views and performance). They do not receive your deposit packet fields from localStorage. You can block scripts with a browser extension; the app still works.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use third-party ad pixels on marketing pages in this release.
  • We do not provide legal advice or review your packets.

Entitlements

After checkout, your browser calls /api/entitlement with your Stripe session id to confirm payment. We return a signed entitlement object stored locally to unlock export.

Retention

Clear site data in your browser to remove local drafts. Stripe retains billing records per their policy.
