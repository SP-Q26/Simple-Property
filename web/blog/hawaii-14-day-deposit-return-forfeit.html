Hawaii · operator pain

You flew back to the mainland before Hawaii’s fourteen-day line closed

Island turnovers compress vendor schedules. Operators who leave itemization for after travel miss the short statutory window.

Wizard default
14 days from surrender (Deposit Desk)
Label
Haw. Rev. Stat. § 521-38

What goes wrong

  • Move-out photos on a phone not in the packet.
  • One cleaning invoice with no room tie-in.
  • Lease end used as surrender.

Fix: Log surrender immediately and export the Hawaii packet before day 14 on screen.
