Louisiana · operator pain

You mailed the balance but skipped the accounting lines Louisiana expects

Mom-and-pop operators sometimes refund the remainder and skip the written breakdown when the withhold feels obvious. That is where tenant disputes start.

Wizard default
30 days from surrender (Deposit Desk)
Label
La. R.S. 9:3251

What goes wrong

  • Carpet and paint merged into one number with no description.
  • Surrender date guessed from lease end.
  • No PDF packet archived for your bank or counsel.

Fix: Export the Deposit Desk packet with itemized lines even when the withhold is small. Same 30-day window on screen from surrender.
