Spreadsheet vs Deposit Desk

Google Sheets and Excel work for one quiet door. They break when three move-outs land the same week and each tenant needs a different surrender date and statute clock.

What searchers usually need

  • Surrender date that starts the clock (not lease end alone) · surrender guide
  • 30 vs 45 days (Illinois statewide vs Chicago RLTO · Indiana 45 · Ohio/Michigan/Iowa/Missouri 30 in the wizard)
  • Line-item withholds, not one cell that says “cleaning” · itemization
  • A filed packet you can mail or email when the tenant disputes · dispute guide

Where spreadsheets fail

  • Wrong row after copy-paste · see Excel mistakes
  • Photos in Drive · numbers in a tab · story in texts · nothing prints as one document
  • No .ics deadline on the calendar you already use

What Deposit Desk adds

  • Five-step wizard · move-in rooms · move-out lines · running total
  • Deadline math from surrender · Chicago toggle on step 1
  • Calendar and Sheets export on free tier · print unlock per turn or Pro at step 5
