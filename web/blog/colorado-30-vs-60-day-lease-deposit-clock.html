Colorado · operator pain

Your Colorado lease said sixty days but you exported on a thirty-day habit

Colorado allows a longer written period up to a cap, while the wizard shows the 30-day default from surrender. Mixing leases across units breaks one column math.

Wizard default
30 days from surrender (Deposit Desk)
Label
C.R.S. § 38-12-103

What goes wrong

  • Lease clause never copied into the operator log.
  • Surrender after lease termination not documented.
  • Itemization mailed without tracking.

Fix: Document surrender, reconcile lease language with counsel, and use Deposit Desk for itemized export discipline on the default clock.
