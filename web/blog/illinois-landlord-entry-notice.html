Entry notice basics

Most Illinois leases require reasonable notice before non-emergency entry. Chicago RLTO tenants often expect stricter habits than a bare statewide lease.

Operator habit that survives disputes:

  1. Write the date, time window, and reason (repair, inspection, showing).
  2. Deliver the way your lease allows (email, text log, posted notice).
  3. Log the contact in the same place you keep maintenance tickets.

Deposit Desk is deposit-first; a contact log lane is on the roadmap · until then, keep a dated note with every entry.

Not legal advice. Read your lease and city code with counsel.
