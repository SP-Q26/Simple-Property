Indiana · deposit desk

Indiana 45-day security deposit return

Indiana
45 days (typical statutory default in Deposit Desk)
Citation
IC 32-31-3-12 et seq.
Start
Surrender · keys returned and unit vacated (confirm locally)

Deposit Desk counts from the surrender date you enter in the wizard, then adds 45 calendar days for Indiana. Local ordinances and lease terms can change your facts · verify with counsel.

What to document

  • Move-in condition notes and dated photos before turnover disputes.
  • Written itemization when you withhold any portion of the deposit.
  • Proof of mailing or delivery if you return balance by mail.
