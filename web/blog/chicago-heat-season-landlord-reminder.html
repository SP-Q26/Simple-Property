Law watch Review each fall · Chicago heat season.

Chicago operators face heat season rules that do not exist the same way in every suburb. Before tenants call 311, test boilers, document service dates, and log no-heat tickets with timestamps.

  • Schedule HVAC service before the first cold snap.
  • Keep vendor invoices in the same folder as lease and deposit files.
  • Separate habitability repairs from deposit withhold lines at move-out.
