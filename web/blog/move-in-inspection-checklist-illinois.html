Illinois security deposit checklist

Thin paperwork turns a routine turnover into a dispute. Use the facts below, then build a dated packet you can mail or email. Not legal advice.

A simple move-in through move-out checklist keeps 30-day math and itemization defensible. Use Deposit Desk for the packet; use operator logs for maintenance and tickets between turns.

Move-in

  • Room-by-room condition · photos · tenant walkthrough when possible.
  • Deposit amount and where it is held (ledger note).

During tenancy

  • Log maintenance and habitability issues (maintenance log).
  • Keep entry and inspection notes (inspections & complaints).

Move-out

  • Record surrender date · start 30-day clock in Deposit Desk.
  • Itemize withholds · mail or deliver remainder with proof.
