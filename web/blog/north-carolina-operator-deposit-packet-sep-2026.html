North Carolina · Simple Property Tools

North Carolina security deposit packet checklist · small landlords

When turnover stacks up, one missing surrender date or move-in photo becomes the story in a deposit dispute. Use the facts below, then open Deposit Desk for a dated packet you can print or hand to counsel. Documentation only · not legal advice.

Itemize withholds, date surrender, and export before the return clock wins. Heavy repair turns may need interim accounting at thirty days even when final work runs longer. Do not go silent.

Return default
30 days from surrender (keys back · unit vacant)
Statute label
N.C.G.S. § 42-52
Practice
One line per withhold · dated photos · proof of mail when you send balance

Before you print or email

  • Log surrender the day possession returns, not the lease end date on paper alone.
  • Match move-in notes to move-out withhold descriptions.
  • Archive the PDF export and tracking number in operator logs.
