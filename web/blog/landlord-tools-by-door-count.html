Landlord tools by door count

Same law at every size · different operational headache. Use the launch stack tables as a checklist.

1–10 doors

You can run on memory until a deposit dispute or missed RLTO deadline proves you cannot. Deploy: Deposit Desk, separate business bank account, per-unit file folder, screening per lease, landlord insurance, and a calendar tied to surrender dates.

10–20 doors

Install systems: rent ledger (Wave, QuickBooks, Stessa), light PM (RentRedi, TenantCloud, Baselane), eSign for leases, maintenance log, and a one-page move-in/move-out SOP your team follows every time.

20–40 doors

Automate rent collection, bank feeds into accounting, PM maintenance tickets, integrated screening · and keep IL deposit itemization honest (PM modules vary; many teams keep a dedicated deposit packet tool).
