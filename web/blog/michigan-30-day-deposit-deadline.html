Michigan 30-day security deposit return

Michigan
30 days (typical statutory default in Deposit Desk)
Citation
MCL 554.610
Start
Surrender · keys returned and unit vacated (confirm locally)

Deposit Desk counts from the surrender date you enter in the wizard, then adds 30 calendar days for Michigan. Local ordinances and lease terms can change your facts · verify with counsel.

What to document

  • Move-in condition notes and dated photos before turnover disputes.
  • Written itemization when you withhold any portion of the deposit.
  • Proof of mailing or delivery if you return balance by mail.
