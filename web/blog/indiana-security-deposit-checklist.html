Indiana security deposit checklist

A simple move-in through move-out checklist keeps 45-day math and itemization defensible. Use Deposit Desk for the packet; use operator logs for maintenance and tickets between turns.

Move-in

  • Room-by-room condition · photos · tenant walkthrough when possible.
  • Deposit amount and where it is held (ledger note).

During tenancy

  • Log maintenance and habitability issues (maintenance log).
  • Keep entry and inspection notes (inspections & complaints).

Move-out

  • Record surrender date · start 45-day clock in Deposit Desk.
  • Itemize withholds · mail or deliver remainder with proof.

IC 32-31-3-12 · confirm local ordinances and lease with counsel.
