Chicago renter deposit timeline

Many Chicago apartments fall under the Residential Landlord Tenant Ordinance (RLTO). One practical difference from suburban leases: landlords often face a 45-day window to return deposit or send itemization after you leave.

Save your move-in photos, lease end date, and proof you returned keys. If you dispute withholdings, the itemization letter matters as much as the clock.
