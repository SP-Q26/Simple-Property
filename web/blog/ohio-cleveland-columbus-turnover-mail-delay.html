Ohio · operator pain

Turnover finished late and the itemized statement went out on day 31

Ohio operators in Cleveland and Columbus often conflate inspection completion with surrender and mail dates.

Wizard default
30 days from surrender (Deposit Desk)
Label
ORC 5321.16

What goes wrong

  • Surrender never entered when keys were picked up.
  • Cleaning invoice arrived after the withhold letter was drafted.
  • No PDF packet saved when the tenant disputes.

Fix: Log surrender at key return, build withhold lines early, export PDF before certified or tracked mail.
