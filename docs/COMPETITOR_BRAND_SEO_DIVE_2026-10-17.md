## Who we are selling to (brand ICP)

| Dimension | Profile |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Self-managing owner or “mom-and-pop” operator · 1–3 people · **1–40 units** (Pro cap) |
| **Trigger** | Turnover weekend · missed surrender date · weak move-in proof · tenant asks for itemization · Chicago vs statewide clock confusion |
| **Job** | Finish a **statutory deposit packet** (deadline + line items + mail-ready export) without adopting a PM suite |
| **Tech** | Spreadsheet today · tolerates Stripe checkout · wants **PDF/CSV** they control |
| **Budget** | **Per-turn** when seasonal · Pro when turns stack · not $50–100/mo suite pricing |
| **Trust** | Greystone / quant palette · plain dealing · receipts and dates · **never** “AI-powered PM” |

**Anti-audience:** institutional PM, deposit-alternative (Obligo/Rhino) buyers, renters shopping lawyers (we link facts, not replace counsel).

## Copy and SEO guardrails

**Say (brand):** Deposit Desk · Condition hub · surrender · itemize · statutory deadline · mail-ready export · per-turn · Pro · 32 states + DC · Chicago RLTO · not legal advice.

**Do not say (our category):** “Property management software” · “Free PM” · “All-in-one platform” · “AI-powered” · Receipt Rail (customer) · PM suite · enterprise.

**Competitor mentions:** Name them fairly; one paragraph max; anchor to **wedge** (clock + packet + price). See `docs/NOMENCLATURE.md`.

**Keywords to grow (editorial):**

- `deposit itemization [state]` · `security deposit return letter` · `wear and tear vs damage deposit`
- `chicago security deposit 45 days` · `765 ILCS 715` operator checklist
- `deposit desk vs [competitor]` · `turnover weekend landlord checklist`
- `condition report security deposit` (hub)

**Avoid in hero/meta:** chasing “best free landlord software” · renter-lawyer voice · penalty scare without **positive** protection frame (`Never pay the fees` beat).
