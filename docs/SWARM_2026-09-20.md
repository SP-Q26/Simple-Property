## Wedge

Inspection SaaS (RentCheck free ≤10 units, Inspect & Cloud ~$49/mo, Hemlane PM bundle) owns **photos + checklists**. Almost none sell **deposit lifecycle**: receipt → surrender date → **30/45-day deadline** → **itemized statement worksheet** → export for mail/email.

**Deposit Desk** = per-lease compliance packet, not property management.

## Illinois deadlines (product logic)

| Jurisdiction | Return / itemize deadline | Primary cite |
|--------------|---------------------------|--------------|
| Illinois (state) | **30 days** after surrender | 765 ILCS **715/** (Security Deposit Return Act) |
| Chicago RLTO | **45 days** | MCC ch. 5-12 (e.g. § 5-12-080) |

App: `cityChicago` toggle → deadline calculator + copy strings. Penalty exposure (e.g. 2× deposit frameworks) cited in footer only — not legal advice.

## Competitor snapshot

1. **RentCheck** — free tier to 10 units; inspection-first.  
2. **Inspect & Cloud** — $49/mo flat; comparison reports; heavy for 1–3 doors.  
3. **Hemlane** — PM suite + checklists; lock-in.  
4. **zInspector / SnapInspect** — per-unit inspection; no IL itemization workflow.  
5. **Forms/Notion** — $0; no diff, deadlines, or dispute-ready packet.
