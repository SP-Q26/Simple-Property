# Brand · Simple Property Tools

**Status:** locked · Homestead mascot · Deposit Desk product · see `CANON.md`.

## Who it’s for

**Mom-and-pop operators** — usually **1–3 people** running a handful of doors, not a property management company. Plain speech, paper you can trust, no “deal OS” theater.

## Mascot · **Homestead**

**Homestead** is a plain line-drawn farmhouse (garden path, split door, sage roof). It stands for **plain dealing**: honest records, no flash, tools that feel like they belong on a kitchen table next to the lease.

We do **not** use ethnic caricature, religious mockery, or “Amish” as a joke. **Amish-adjacent** here means **values**: simple, local, careful craftsmanship, understated UI — the same lane as plain dress and barn-raising *ethos*, not costume.

## Voice

- Short sentences. No hype.
- “Packet,” “receipt,” “deadline,” “keep a copy.”
- Sign-off energy: *Built for small teams who still answer their own phone.*
