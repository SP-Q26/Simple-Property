# Voice research · Reddit & Quora · Deposit Desk

Not legal advice. Patterns from public threads (tenant-heavy on Reddit; operators on Quora and r/Landlord).

## Tenant complaints (what operators get sued/texted about)

| Theme | What people say | Deposit Desk answer |
|-------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **No itemized list** | Deposit kept with no written breakdown, or only after repeated asks | Step 4 line items · print packet |
| **Vague lump sums** | Text: "applied to cleaning, paint, carpet" with no amounts or receipts | Separate lines per withhold · not one cell |
| **Missed statutory clock** | "Still preparing accounting" past 30/21/14 days | Surrender date · deadline on step 5 · calendar export |
| **Wear vs damage fights** | Carpet replacement, "full cost" vs depreciation (Quora) | Move-in photos · wear vs damage guides |
| **Spreadsheet chaos** | r/Landlord: sheets break at scale; tabs for everything | One packet per turn · not PM suite |

## Tagline (shipped)

**Primary:** `Itemize it. Date it. Keep the clock.`

- **Itemize it** · answers "no itemized list" / vague text threads  
- **Date it** · surrender + return deadline  
- **Keep the clock** · calendar/ics · statute window visible  

**Header sub-tag:** `Deposit Desk · itemized · clock · IL · IN · OH · MI · IA · MO`

**Hero H1 unchanged:** `Spreadsheets break at surrender date. The clock does not.`
